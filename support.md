## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I add the widget to my Home Screen?

**iOS:**

1. Long-press an empty area of your Home Screen
2. Tap the **+** button in the top-left corner
3. Search for **Nice Words**
4. Pick a widget size (small, medium, or large)
5. Tap **Add Widget**

**Android:**

1. Long-press an empty area of your Home Screen
2. Tap **Widgets** in the menu
3. Find **Nice Words** in the list
4. Long-press the widget and drag it onto your Home Screen

### How do I turn off ads?

Go to **Profile → Manage Subscription** and choose a plan. Cancel anytime from your device's subscription settings.

### How do I set up daily reminders?

**Profile → Notifications** — pick the affirmation category, choose a time, save.

### How do I create my own affirmations?

Open the **Affirmations Library** tab, tap a category (or create a new one), then **+ Add affirmation**.

### The Shake-for-Affirmation feature isn't working — what should I check?

Go to **Profile → Settings → Shake for affirmation** and make sure the toggle is on. You also need at least one affirmation saved. Make sure your device isn't in Low Power Mode, which can dampen shake detection.

### How do I delete my account?

**Profile → My Account → Delete Account.** This permanently removes your data from our servers and cannot be undone.

### How do I change the app language?

**Profile → App Language** — choose from English, German, Spanish, French, Polish, or Portuguese (Brazil).

### I have a subscription question — refunds, cancellations, billing

Subscriptions are managed by Apple / Google. To cancel or request a refund:

- **iOS:** Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions → Nice Words → Cancel
- **Android:** Play Store → Menu → Subscriptions → Nice Words → Cancel

We can't process refunds directly — please contact Apple Support or Google Play Support.

### The app crashed or a feature isn't working

Email us at [contact.nicewords@gmail.com](mailto:contact.nicewords@gmail.com) with:

- Your device model + OS version
- What you were doing when it crashed
- A screenshot if possible

We take crashes seriously and usually ship a fix within a few days.
