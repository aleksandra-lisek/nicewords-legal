## 2. Types of Data Collected

We collect the following categories of personal data:

**Account data** — email address, display name, profile picture (if provided via Google/Apple sign-in), user identifier.

**User-generated content** — affirmation groups, custom badges, custom tasks, daily task completions, application settings and preferences.

**Device and usage data** — device model, operating system version, application version, language, time zone, crash reports, performance metrics, in-app interactions ("Usage Data").

**Advertising identifiers** — Apple IDFA (iOS) or Google Advertising ID (Android), collected only with user consent via the platform's ATT/UMP consent flow.

**Purchase data** — subscription status, purchase history, product identifiers. No card or payment details are ever handled by the App; all payments are processed by Apple or Google.

**Push notification tokens** — device tokens used to deliver notifications, only if the user grants notification permission.

### Whether provision of data is mandatory

- **Email address** — mandatory to create an account; without it you cannot use synced features. Without an account, you can still use limited offline functionality.
- **Analytics and advertising data** — optional; refusal has no impact on the App's core functionality.
- **Push notification tokens** — optional; refusal means you will not receive reminders or notifications.
- **Advertising identifier (IDFA/AAID)** — optional; refusal means you will see non-personalized ads instead of personalized ones (free tier only).

## 3. How We Use Your Data

We process personal data for the following purposes:

- **Providing the service** — authenticating you, storing your affirmations and settings, syncing across devices
- **Improving the app** — pseudonymous usage analytics, crash diagnostics
- **Delivering notifications** — daily affirmation reminders, streak notifications
- **Processing subscriptions** — tracking active premium entitlements
- **Serving advertisements** — showing personalized or non-personalized ads (based on your consent choice) to free-tier users
- **Complying with legal obligations**

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the EU / EEA, we process personal data under the following legal bases:

- **Consent** — analytics, advertising, personalized notifications, push notifications
- **Performance of a contract** — account management, subscription processing, providing the service
- **Legitimate interest** — see below
- **Legal obligation** — where required by applicable law

### Specific legitimate interests we pursue

Where we rely on legitimate interest as a legal basis, our specific interests are:

- **Product improvement:** analyzing pseudonymous usage patterns to identify bugs, improve features, and prioritize development. Balanced against your privacy by using pseudonymized data only.
- **Fraud and abuse prevention:** using Firebase App Check to prevent unauthorized API access and protect legitimate users from service disruption.
- **Security monitoring:** using crash reports to identify and fix security vulnerabilities.

You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interest — see Section 7 below.

### Automated decision-making

We do not use your personal data for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you.

## 6. Data Retention

- **Account data** — retained for as long as your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion.
- **User-generated content** — retained while the account is active. Deleted immediately upon account deletion; we do not maintain user-recoverable backups.
- **Analytics data** — retained by Google for up to 14 months.
- **Crash reports** — retained by Sentry for up to 90 days.
- **Purchase history** — retained by RevenueCat and Apple/Google according to their policies; typically **7 years** for tax and accounting compliance in Poland.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Nice Words is not intended for children.

- **Users in the EU/EEA:** the App is not intended for users under **16**. For transfers to the United States, we rely on:

1. **EU-US Data Privacy Framework certification** — Google LLC is certified: https://www.dataprivacyframework.gov/list

## 12. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

- All network communication is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS
- Local database encryption on-device (SQLCipher / AES-256)
- Firebase App Check to prevent abuse
- Firestore security rules restricting access to authorized users only
- Regular review of dependencies for security vulnerabilities

In the event of a personal data breach likely to result in a high risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you without undue delay via the email address associated with your account, as required by GDPR Art. 34, and notify the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours as required by Art. 33.
